Our Chapter

The Next Step in Your Masonic Journey.

Tradition, companionship and discovery in one of England’s most historic cities.

Wolvesey Chapter No. 6818 brings together Royal Arch Freemasons, known as Companions, to continue the journey begun in the Craft. The Chapter is part of the Hampshire & Isle of Wight Royal Arch community.

Whether you are an existing Companion planning a visit, or a Master Mason interested in discovering more about the Royal Arch, we would be pleased to help you find out more.

The Royal Arch.

Further light, fresh perspectives and lifelong friendship.

The Royal Arch is an integral part of English Freemasonry and continues the story begun in the three Craft degrees. Through its distinctive ceremony of Exaltation, Master Masons explore new perspectives on the principles and teachings of the Craft.
