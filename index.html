The Royal Arch.

Further light, fresh perspectives and lifelong friendship.

The Royal Arch is an integral part of English Freemasonry and continues the story begun in the three Craft degrees. Through its distinctive ceremony of Exaltation, Master Masons explore new perspectives on the principles and teachings of the Craft.

How to join

Good to know.

When does Wolvesey Chapter meet?

The published regular pattern is the second Wednesday in February, May, October (Installation) and December. Dates shown here are indicative; the summons confirms the final details.

Where is Wolvesey Chapter based?

Wolvesey is listed in the Winchester area. A Masonic directory records Winchester Masonic Centre, 124 Alresford Road, Winchester, SO21 1HB as the Chapter's meeting place. Please confirm current arrangements before travelling.

How should I arrange a visit?

Please contact the Chapter ahead of time using its Facebook page or your existing Chapter contact. A public calendar date is not a confirmed invitation.

Who is eligible to join the Royal Arch?

Under the English Constitution, a Master Mason of at least four weeks' standing in a Lodge under UGLE, or another Grand Lodge recognised by UGLE, may apply. See the official guidance.

Sources: Hampshire & Isle of Wight Province, Masonic centre directory and Supreme Grand Chapter.
