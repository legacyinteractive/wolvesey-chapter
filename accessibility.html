Accessibility

We aim to make the Wolvesey Chapter website usable with a keyboard, screen readers, and a range of desktop and mobile screen sizes.

Site features

Navigation links and buttons are keyboard-accessible, text and controls have visible focus styling, and layouts adapt to smaller screens. The public website also respects the operating system’s reduced-motion setting.

Known limitations

Some imagery and fonts are hosted by third parties. The Members Area shown on the homepage is a non-functional preview and no private documents are published through the public site.

Report a problem

If you find an accessibility problem or need information in another format, please reach us using the Chapter contact links, and include the page and problem encountered.
