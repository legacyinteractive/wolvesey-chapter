Website concept review

A proposed new home for Wolvesey Chapter.

This working demonstration is for feedback and a decision about progressing towards a full production website. It has not been formally approved for launch.

What the concept includes

Design and identity

A responsive Royal Arch design using the Chapter logo, red, black and white styling, a Chapter photograph and clear navigation.

Public information

An introduction to the Chapter, Royal Arch information, visitor and joining guidance, indicative meetings and a link to Chapter social media.

What remains provisional

  • History, news, named officers and official contact information require Chapter confirmation and editorial approval.
  • Meeting dates and a directory-recorded venue are indicative; the latest Chapter summons is authoritative.
  • Some Winchester photographs are illustrative external images rather than Chapter photography, subject to replacement or approval.
  • The Members Area is not active. It shows information only and does not grant access or process member data.
  • A permanent domain, security, privacy approval and ongoing maintenance would need agreement before production.

Questions for your review

Does the appearance reflect the Chapter? Is the content clear and welcoming? Would the website benefit existing and future Companions? Are there corrections or additions needed? Would you support developing this into a fully approved production website?

To give feedback: please reply directly to the person who sent you this link. There is no feedback form on this demonstration. Routine technical requests may be processed by the hosting provider; please see the privacy information.
