Privacy Information

This page explains how the public Wolvesey Chapter website works. The site does not currently offer a contact form, newsletter registration, private login, or online payment facility.

Information submitted to us

If you contact the Chapter through Facebook or X, those services process your messages according to their own privacy policies. Please avoid sending confidential membership details through public social-media posts.

Website delivery and external resources

This site is delivered through Cloudflare, which may process technical information such as IP addresses to operate and protect the website. The public site also loads Google Fonts and selected Wikimedia Commons images; requests to those services may disclose technical connection information to their providers.

Cookies

Our current public website code does not set optional advertising or analytics cookies. Third-party websites linked from our pages have their own practices. This notice will be updated if additional services are introduced.

Contact

For a privacy question, please contact Wolvesey Chapter through the Chapter contact links.
